# Knowledge work

He turns what he reads into his own notes. For years that was one append-and-review note; since 2025 it is reading with an LLM in passes and having the LLM compile a personal wiki he can question.
  > "a large fraction of my recent token throughput is going less into manipulating code, and more into manipulating knowledge (stored as markdown and images)." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2039805659525644595) · 2026-04

## Evidence

- He keeps exactly one note, with no folders.
  > "I maintain one single text note in the Apple Notes app just called" — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03
  > "Maintaining more than one note and managing and sorting them into folders and recursive substructures costs way too much cognitive bloat." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03
- He reviews it by scrolling and copying what matters back to the top; the rest sinks.
  > "If I find anything that deserves to not leave my attention, I rescue it towards the top by simply copy pasting." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03
  > "Notes that repeatedly don't deserve attention will naturally continue to sink." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03
- Writing a thing down lets him clear his head.
  > "When I note something down, I feel that I can immediately move on, wipe my working memory, and focus fully on something else at that time." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03
- He reads everything with an LLM, in three passes.
  > "Usually pass 1 is manual, then pass 2 "explain/summarize", pass 3 Q&A. I usually end up with a better/deeper understanding than if I moved on." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1990577951671509438) · 2025-11
  > "it is rarely ever the case anymore that I read books just by myself I always involve an LM to help me read a book" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3301s) · 2025-02
- The LLM keeps the wiki; he does not edit it by hand.
  > "Obsidian is the IDE; the LLM is the programmer; the wiki is the codebase." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
  > "You rarely ever write or edit the wiki manually, it's the domain of the LLM." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2039805659525644595) · 2026-04
- He stays involved: one source at a time, checking the summaries, and filing good answers back.
  > "Personally I prefer to ingest sources one at a time and stay involved — I read the summaries, check the updates, and guide the LLM on what to emphasize." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
  > "good answers can be filed back into the wiki as new pages." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
- Each new view of the same facts gives him insight.
  > "So, it's really just a lot of prompts for me to do synthetic data generation kind of over over some fixed data." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1735s) · 2026-04
  > "anytime I see a different projection onto information, I always like feel like I gain insight." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1732s) · 2026-04

## How it shows up

- He starts a chapter with a summary, then reads it himself.
  > "usually I like to start off with a summary of what this chapter is about just so I have a rough idea and then I go in and I start reading the chapter" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3444s) · 2025-02
- He built a reader that makes chapters easy to paste into an LLM.
  > "This makes it very easy to copy paste the contents of a chapter to an LLM, to read along." — [github/reader3/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/reader3/blob/64960f99d8d3c2eaaec56c6765b4c1aeae14c80b/README.md) · 2025-11
- He hands the LLM the bookkeeping and keeps the questions.
  > "The human's job is to curate sources, direct the analysis, ask good questions, and think about what it all means. The LLM's job is everything else." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
- He argues both sides with the model to avoid being flattered into a view.
  > "This is actually super useful as a tool for forming your own opinions, just make sure to ask different directions and be careful with the sycophancy." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2037921699824607591) · 2026-03
- When typing is too slow, he rambles to the model by voice.
  > "One pattern I find useful for working with LLMs is a nice long ramble session." — [x/2026-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2079610838143623371) · 2026-07
- He reaches past popular books to textbooks and paper appendices, and on young research directions he reads only the abstracts, because those papers are still just ideas.
  > "I would say sometimes books are not sufficient. I like to reach for textbooks sometimes." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=11515s) · 2022-10
  > "Honestly, I find papers are quite good. I especially like the appendix of any paper as well. It's like the most detail you can have." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=11663s) · 2022-10
  > "I am only at a stage of reading abstracts because a lot of these papers are just ideas." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=3000s) · 2025-10
- He records experiments in the note too.
  > "I'm running a hyperparameter sweep of my neural network and I record the commands I ran and the eventual outcome of the experiment." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/) · 2025-03

## Tensions and evolution

- In 2018 he decided that reading a book is not learning, and turned to textbooks. By 2025 reading with an LLM had become one of his main uses of AI.
  > "It took me a while to really admit to myself that just reading a book is not learning but entertainment." — [x/2018-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1018544870112165888) · 2018-07
  > "Discovering (paradoxically late in life) that I get more out of textbooks than books and that I don't have to stop buying them just because I'm out of school." — [x/2018-08.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1031266180240093184) · 2018-08
  > "Growing to among top use cases." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1990577951671509438) · 2025-11
- In 2016 his notes were scattered: paper notes in a repo he wasn't sure about, and readmes written for his future self. In 2026 an LLM keeps them in one wiki.
  > "Random notes? There should be a better place for this I think" — [github/paper-notes/Readme.md](https://github.com/karpathy/paper-notes/blob/75f4b42f3630252e86728bd78572a666c5f4de1a/Readme.md) · 2016-08
  > "I got into the habit of creating very thorough readme.txt files in all my repos (for my personal use) as notes to future self on how the code works, how to run it, etc." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
  > "This works surprisingly well at moderate scale (~100 sources, ~hundreds of pages) and avoids the need for embedding-based RAG infrastructure." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
- He no longer shares the wiki code, only the idea for others' agents to build.
  > "Its goal is to communicate the high level idea, but your agent will build out the specifics in collaboration with you." — [github/_gists/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f/llm-wiki.md](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) · 2026-04
