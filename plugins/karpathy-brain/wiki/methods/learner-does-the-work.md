# Learner does the work

He makes learners predict, guess and build the pieces themselves, with the video as the answer key.
  > "I recommend you work through the exercise yourself but work with it in tandem and whenever you are stuck unpause the video and see me give away the answer. This video is not super intended to be simply watched." — [github/nn-zero-to-hero/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/nn-zero-to-hero/blob/73c3fcc741f0ec104ca850b1fb0df90e7e8d4cde/README.md) · 2024-08

## Evidence

- He stops the video to ask the viewer to predict or solve before he does.
  > "do you expect do you expect f of x plus h to be slightly greater than 20 or do you expect to be slightly lower than 20" — [youtube/2022-10-09_VMj-3S1tku0_the-spelled-out-intro-to-neural.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0&t=705s) · 2022-08
  > "this is the exercise and maybe pause the video here and see if you can figure it out yourself as to what we should put here" — [youtube/2022-10-09_VMj-3S1tku0_the-spelled-out-intro-to-neural.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VMj-3S1tku0&t=5655s) · 2022-08
  > "I'd like you to potentially pause the video Even and think through why one of these two is significantly better answer" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=6483s) · 2025-02
- Guessing first is a principle for him: it makes each fact worth more.
  > "Always prompting the student. There's a lot of small things like that are important and a lot of good educators will do this. How would you solve this? I'm not going to present the solution before you guess. That would be wasteful." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8411s) · 2025-10
  > "It maximizes the amount of knowledge per new fact added." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8458s) · 2025-10
- In CS231n students wrote every backward pass by hand on purpose.
  > "we intentionally designed the programming assignments to include explicit calculations involved in backpropagation on the lowest level" — [blog/karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b.md](https://karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b) · 2016-12
  > "The students had to implement the forward and the backward pass of each layer in raw numpy." — [blog/karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b.md](https://karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b) · 2016-12
- He publishes exercise progressions that end in a full reimplementation.
  > "For those trying to study BPE, here is the advised progression exercise for how you can build your own minbpe step by step." — [github/minbpe/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/minbpe/blob/1acefe89412b20245db5a22d2a02001e547dc602/README.md) · 2024-02
  > "I've published the this uh exercise progression that you can follow" — [youtube/2024-10-09_zduSFxRajkE_let-s-build-the-gpt-tokenizer.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zduSFxRajkE&t=5149s) · 2024-02
  > "work in Tandem and try it first yourself and then see me give away the answer and I think that'll be most valuable to you" — [youtube/2023-10-09_q8SA3rM6ckI_building-makemore-part-4-becoming-a.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8SA3rM6ckI&t=770s) · 2022-10
- He sees exercises and notes as ways to work the material.
  > "there's exercises in the textbook what are those those are prompts to you to exercise the material right" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_c3b-JASoPi0_making-ai-accessible-with-andrej-karpathy.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3b-JASoPi0&t=1702s) · 2024-03
  > "maybe you're taking notes you're rephrasing reframing like you're doing a lot of manipulation of this knowledge in a way of you like learning that knowledge" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_c3b-JASoPi0_making-ai-accessible-with-andrej-karpathy.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c3b-JASoPi0&t=1713s) · 2024-03
- Explaining something is how he learns it, and he wants learners to do the same.
  > "it definitely strengthens your understanding. So it's not a purely altruistic activity, it's a way to learn. If you have to explain something to someone, you realize you have gaps in knowledge." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9667s) · 2022-10

- CS231n was about putting a working tool into many hands.
  > "It's somewhat like a hammer and what I wanted to do I was in a position to randomly kind of hand out this hammer to a lot of people and I just found that very compelling" — [youtube-appearances/unknown__au3yw46lcg_heroes-of-deep-learning-andrew-ng.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_au3yw46lcg&t=389s) · 2017-08
  > "But I felt like it was probably more impactful than getting a paper or two out to actually like do this class and do a good job of it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_PlLmdTcsAWU_season-1-ep-1-andrej-karpathy.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PlLmdTcsAWU&t=433s) · 2021-09

## How it shows up

- He gives the learner a turn before pasting his own code.
  > "try to implement this function yourself it's a fun exercise otherwise I'm going to start uh pasting in my own solution" — [youtube/2024-10-09_zduSFxRajkE_let-s-build-the-gpt-tokenizer.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zduSFxRajkE&t=2585s) · 2024-02
- Exercises come with a check against the real thing.
  > "You're now ready to load the merges from the GPT-4 tokenizer and show that your tokenizer produces the identical results for both `encode` and `decode`, matching" — [github/minbpe/exercise.md](https://github.com/karpathy/minbpe/blob/1acefe89412b20245db5a22d2a02001e547dc602/exercise.md) · 2024-02
  > "(Optional, irritating, not obviously useful) Add the ability to handle special tokens." — [github/minbpe/exercise.md](https://github.com/karpathy/minbpe/blob/1acefe89412b20245db5a22d2a02001e547dc602/exercise.md) · 2024-02
- He sends learners to the primary paper once they can read it.
  > "It is 19 pages, but at this point you should actually be able to read a good chunk of this paper and understand uh pretty good chunks of it." — [youtube/2022-10-09_TCH_1BHY58I_building-makemore-part-2-mlp.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCH_1BHY58I&t=4377s) · 2022-09
- He points them at the notebook so they can work alongside.
  > "I think would be most pedagogically useful for you is to actually go into the description of this video and find the link to this Jupiter notebook" — [youtube/2023-10-09_q8SA3rM6ckI_building-makemore-part-4-becoming-a.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8SA3rM6ckI&t=748s) · 2022-10
- He prefers learning on demand, pulled by a project.
  > "But I love the kind of learning where you'll get a reward out of doing something, and you're learning on demand." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8690s) · 2025-10
  > "when you're working on a project you know what you need and you're learning it on demand and you're just trying to get it to work" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_tsTeEkzO9xc_andrej-karpathy-s-keynote-winner-pitches.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tsTeEkzO9xc&t=1238s) · 2024-07
- He learns a craft the same way: see many good and bad examples and train a classifier in your head.
  > "What you really want is to also have exposure to a large number of bad papers and one way to get this is by reviewing papers." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
  > "Don’t just enjoy talks; analyze them, break them down, learn from them." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09

## Tensions and evolution

- In 2025 he wrote that AI breaks take-home work: schools cannot detect it, so the doing has to move into class.
  > "You will never be able to detect the use of AI in homework. Full stop." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1993010584175141038) · 2025-11
  > "the majority of grading has to shift to in-class work (instead of at-home assignments), in settings where teachers can physically monitor students." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1993010584175141038) · 2025-11
