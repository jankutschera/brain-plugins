# Playful and accessible

He keeps it fun and low-friction: toy datasets, browser demos, Colab, humor.
  > "If you are not a deep learning professional and you just want to feel the magic and get your feet wet" — [github/nanoGPT/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT/blob/3adf61e154c3fe3fca428ad6bc3818b27a3b8291/README.md) · 2022-12

## Tensions and evolution

- His lessons are playful, yet in 2024 he wrote that learning should feel like effort, not fun.
  > "Learning is not supposed to be fun. It doesn't have to be actively not fun either, but the primary feeling should be that of effort." — [x/2024-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1756380066580455557) · 2024-02
- By 2023 he had stopped maintaining ConvNetJS, his 2014 browser library.
  > "I am not actively maintaining ConvNetJS anymore because I simply don't have time." — [github/convnetjs/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/convnetjs/blob/4c3358a315b4d71f31a0d532eb5d1700e9e592ee/README.md) · 2023-01
