# Education with AI

He sees AI as scaling the tutor, while the human teacher designs the material; that was the idea behind Eureka Labs.
  > "The teacher still designs the course materials, but they are supported, leveraged and scaled with an AI Teaching Assistant who is optimized to help guide the students through them." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07

## Evidence

- Eureka Labs was meant to be an AI-native school, because great teachers are scarce.
  > "We are Eureka Labs and we are building a new kind of school that is AI native." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07
  > "subject matter experts who are deeply passionate, great at teaching, infinitely patient and fluent in all of the world's languages are also very scarce" — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07
- The teacher moves to the back end and the AI becomes the front end.
  > "the teacher is not the front end anymore the teacher is on the back end designing the materials in the course and the AI is the front end" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=1975s) · 2024-09
  > "how do you use AI to serve like do the scaling of a really good teacher" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=1955s) · 2024-09
- The course is an auditable artifact, and the AI stays on its leash.
  > "we now have this intermediate artifact of a course that is auditable and we can make sure it's good." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_LCEmiRjPEtQ_andrej-karpathy-software-is-changing-again.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ&t=1526s) · 2025-06
  > "the AI is kept on the leash with respect to a certain syllabus, a certain like um progression of projects and so on." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_LCEmiRjPEtQ_andrej-karpathy-software-is-changing-again.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ&t=1533s) · 2025-06
- His bar for a tutor is a human who reads the student at once and keeps them challenged; LLMs are not there yet.
  > "I need to be always appropriately challenged. I can't be faced with something too hard or too trivial, and a tutor is really good at serving you just the right stuff." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7252s) · 2025-10
  > "No LLM will do that for you 100% right now, not even close." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7241s) · 2025-10
  > "how far can a person go if they have the perfect tutor for all the subjects" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=1881s) · 2024-09
- He works on education to keep humans empowered, which he sees as his unique contribution.
  > "I want humans to be well off in the future. I feel like that's where I can a lot more uniquely add value than an incremental improvement in the frontier lab." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7075s) · 2025-10
  > "I'm always uh more interested in anything that kind of empowers people and I feel like I'm kind of on a high level like team human" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=1858s) · 2024-09
- He teaches for leverage and for happy learners, not for love of the act.
  > "Yeah, I mean I don't think I love teaching. I love happy humans and happy humans like when I teach." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9563s) · 2022-10
  > "I feel like it's such a larger lever over, you know, kind of impact. It's indirect, but I think it's a very large lever." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_PlLmdTcsAWU_season-1-ep-1-andrej-karpathy.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PlLmdTcsAWU&t=365s) · 2021-09

## How it shows up

- He uses LLMs to build course material faster but does the creative part himself.
  > "I feel like I'm developing the course much faster, and it's LLM-infused, but it's not yet at a place where it can creatively create the content. I'm still there to do that." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7540s) · 2025-10
- He now writes skills that tell agents how to teach.
  > "basically skill is just a way to instruct the agent how to teach the thing." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kwSVtQ7dziU_skill-issue-andrej-karpathy-on-code.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU&t=3825s) · 2026-03
  > "Teacher input is the unique sliver of contribution that the AI can't make yet (but usually already easily understands when given)." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2030387285250994192) · 2026-03
- He publishes in forms an LLM can read, so readers can ask it questions.
  > "These can be useful for both people who may want to just read the summary in text format, but also for LLMs so that my content is legible and available to them." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/) · 2026-04
  > "should you wish to fork the conversation and ask any questions with all of the context" — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/animals-vs-ghosts.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/animals-vs-ghosts/) · 2025-10
  > "it can then target, personalize and serve the idea to its user." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1990577951671509438) · 2025-11
- He reads hard books with an LLM and reports better retention.
  > "going hand inand with llms uh dramatically creases my retention my understanding of these chapters" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3457s) · 2025-02
- The content is free; the business is everything around it.
  > "My default thinking is that the content itself is free and permissively licensed, the revenue comes from everything else" — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813273726441652683) · 2024-07
- He thinks early schooling is for booting up a brain, and physics does that best.
  > "Children should learn physics in early education not because they go on to do physics, but because it is the subject that best boots up a brain." — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1979644538185752935) · 2025-10
  > "early school education is not about accumulating knowledge or memory for tasks later in the industry. It's about booting up a brain." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8170s) · 2025-10
- He wants learning to alternate depth on demand with breadth.
  > "I do feel you need a bit of alternation of learning depth-wise, on demand" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8671s) · 2025-10
- The goal is the moment of understanding.
  > "is the awesome feeling of understanding something, of feeling it click. The goal here is to spark those moments in people's minds." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813277222964502686) · 2024-07

## Tensions and evolution

- In 2024 he called an AI front end to a human-made course tractable; in 2025 he said a tutor as good as a strong human one was not yet buildable, so for now he was building something more conventional.
  > "I think that's tractable today uh and it just doesn't exist and I think it can be made really good" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=1998s) · 2024-09
  > "That's why I think it's not actually the right time to build this kind of an AI tutor." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7281s) · 2025-10
  > "For now, I'm building something that looks maybe a bit more conventional that has a physical and digital component and so on." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7341s) · 2025-10
- In 2026 he saw himself losing the edge as an explainer to the models.
  > "I'm not explaining to people anymore. I'm explaining it to agents." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kwSVtQ7dziU_skill-issue-andrej-karpathy-on-code.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU&t=3789s) · 2026-03
  > "I still think I can probably explain things a little bit better than the agents, but I still feel like the models are improving so rapidly that um I feel like it's a losing battle to some to some extent." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kwSVtQ7dziU_skill-issue-andrej-karpathy-on-code.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU&t=3856s) · 2026-03
- LLM101n grew in scope and was still unfinished in late 2025.
  > "NOTE: this course does not yet exist. It is current being developed by" — [github/LLM101n/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/LLM101n/blob/c6de374acd4b57b6fea3b1a1b6e945d58f19cd30/README.md) · 2024-08
  > "LLM101n is currently experiencing a lot of scope creep" — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1977763719812710501) · 2025-10
  > "nanochat will be the capstone project of LLM101n (which is still being developed)." — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1977755427569111362) · 2025-10
- Teaching went from a side quest (until 2024) to a full-time company (2024-07) to a pause when he joined Anthropic (2026-05).
  > "All of my work combining the two so far has only been part-time, as side quests to my "real job"" — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07
  > "so I am quite excited to dive in and build something great, professionally and full-time." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07
