# Ambition and agency

Pick ambitious problems, because a 10x more important problem is not 10x harder, and count agency and sheer volume of work above raw intelligence.
  > "a 10x more important problem is at most 2-3x harder to achieve" — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09

## Evidence

- He calls this sublinear scaling of difficulty, and repeats it years after the PhD essay.
  > "I think there's a, what I call sublinear scaling of difficulty, which means that 10x problems are not 10x hard." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=5858s) · 2022-10
- A 10x goal works because it forces a different approach.
  > "It’s because thinking 10x forces you out of the box, to confront the real limitations of an approach, to think from first principles, to change the strategy completely, to innovate." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
  > "And it's because you fundamentally change the approach. And if you start with that constraint, then some approaches are obviously dumb and not going to work and it forces you to reevaluate." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=5879s) · 2022-10
- He draws a line between ambitious and impossible.
  > "I wouldn't say that setting impossible goals exactly is a good idea but I think setting very ambitious goals is a good idea." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=5852s) · 2022-10
- Most of research is choosing the problem, and incremental work is the trap.
  > "You spend most of your time on the outer loop, figuring out what problems are worth solving and what problems are ripe for solving." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
  > "Incremental work is a paper that enhances something existing by making it more complex and gets 2% extra on some benchmark." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
- He tells people to ignore the proxy metrics and do what the field needs.
  > "Think for yourself and from first principles. Do things others don’t do but should. Step off the treadmill that has been put before you." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
  > "if you try to game any of the proxy metrics you won’t be successful in the long run" — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
- Agency beats intelligence.
  > "Agency is significantly more powerful and significantly more scarce." — [x/2025-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1894099637218545984) · 2025-02
- Volume of work matters more than choosing perfectly, and the only fair comparison is with your past self.
  > "Beginners are often focused on what to do and I think the focus should be more how much you do." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9396s) · 2022-10
  > "You literally have to put in 10,000 hours of work. It doesn't even matter as much where you put it, you'll iterate and you'll improve and you'll waste some time." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9412s) · 2022-10
  > "only compare yourself to younger you, never to others" — [x/2020-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1325154823856033793) · 2020-11
- In 2026 he says what frontier models do for you scales with the ambition and size of the problems you bring them.
  > "The impact scales with ambition, problem size, and horizon. A question with a paragraph answer barely stresses the system." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "You need a reservoir of big, difficult problems that you really care about." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10

## How it shows up

- His own example of a 10x constraint: it rules out tuning the old method.
  > "Well, it's not probably by tuning a HOG feature detector, I need a different approach. I need something that is scalable." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=5947s) · 2022-10
- As a PhD student he put far more into teaching CS231n than was rational for someone who should be doing research, because he judged the field was held back by too few people who could enter it.
  > "I put much more effort into it than is rationally advisable for a PhD student who should be doing research, but I felt that the field was held back if people couldn’t efficiently learn about the topic and enter." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
- He tells students that failed work still counts.
  > "You will accumulate scar tissue and next time you'll grow stronger because next time you'll have the scar tissue, and next time you'll learn from it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9509s) · 2022-10
- He tells hackers to count hours, not worry about results.
  > "Just do simple B counting of how many hours you're going to you're doing and everything adds up." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_tsTeEkzO9xc_andrej-karpathy-s-keynote-winner-pitches.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tsTeEkzO9xc&t=1001s) · 2024-07
- He trusts that good work gets noticed without gaming.
  > "Do good work, communicate it properly, people will notice and good things will happen." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
- He values a physical, hands-on sense of computers over the mathematical view.
  > "And too little on physical lens - energy/heat of state change, data locality, parallelism, computer architecture. The former is interesting; The latter bestows power." — [x/2025-05.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1919647115099451892) · 2025-05

## Tensions and evolution

- He says he got the agency point wrong for most of his career.
  > "I had this intuitively wrong for decades" — [x/2025-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1894099637218545984) · 2025-02
- He looks back on his undergrad focus on math as a mistake.
  > "A major mistake I made in my undergrad is that I focused way too much on mathematical lens of computing" — [x/2025-05.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1919647115099451892) · 2025-05
- Independence versus the frontier. Outside the labs he could speak freely but worried his judgment would drift. In May 2026 he joined Anthropic.
  > "You're you're not a completely free agent and you can't actually like be part of that conversation in a fully autonomous um free way." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kwSVtQ7dziU_skill-issue-andrej-karpathy-on-code.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU&t=2751s) · 2026-03
  > "if you're outside of that frontier lab, your your judgment fundamentally will start to drift because you're not part of the you know, what's coming down the line." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kwSVtQ7dziU_skill-issue-andrej-karpathy-on-code.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwSVtQ7dziU&t=2844s) · 2026-03
  > "Personal update: I've joined Anthropic." — [x/2026-05.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312) · 2026-05
  > "I think the next few years at the frontier of LLMs will be especially formative." — [x/2026-05.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312) · 2026-05
