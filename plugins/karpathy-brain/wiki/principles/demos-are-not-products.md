# Demos are not products

A demo is cheap and a product is a march of nines, so autonomy arrives gradually and timelines need calibrating against the hype.
  > "What takes the long amount of time and the way to think about it is that it's a march of nines. Every single nine is a constant amount of work." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=6359s) · 2025-10

## Evidence

- Many tasks have a large gap between an easy demo and a hard product, so demos do not impress him.
  > "For some kinds of tasks and jobs and so on, there's a very large demo-to-product gap where the demo is very easy, but the product is very hard." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=6294s) · 2025-10
  > "I'm very unimpressed by demos. Whenever I see demos of anything, I'm extremely unimpressed by that." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=6391s) · 2025-10
- He learned this from self-driving. A perfect Waymo demo drive in 2013 made it feel imminent; at Tesla, customers then forced the team through the long tail.
  > "at the time when I had this perfect drive, this perfect demo, I felt like, wow, self-driving is imminent because this just worked." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_LCEmiRjPEtQ_andrej-karpathy-software-is-changing-again.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ&t=1612s) · 2025-06
  > "because this is a product in the hands of customers you are forced to go through the longtail you can't just do 95% and call it a day" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_g2R2T631x7k_cvpr-20-workshop-on-scalability-in.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g2R2T631x7k&t=684s) · 2020-06
- His own vibe-coded app went the same way: a fun local demo, a slog to deploy.
  > "Vibe coding menugen was exhilarating and fun escapade as a local demo, but a bit of a painful slog as a deployed, real app." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/) · 2025-04
  > "vibe coding full web apps today is kind of messy and not a good idea for anything of actual importance" — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/) · 2025-04
- He expects automating software work to look like automating driving: a decade of agents.
  > "In my mind, automating software engineering will look similar to automating driving." — [x/2024-03.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1767598414945292695) · 2024-03
  > "Personally I think 2025-2035 is the decade of agents." — [x/2025-01.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1882544526033924438) · 2025-01
- He calibrates against the hype on purpose, and says his timelines are longer than the crowd's.
  > "when I see things like oh 2025 is the year of agents I get very concerned and I kind of feel like you know this is the decade of agents" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_LCEmiRjPEtQ_andrej-karpathy-software-is-changing-again.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ&t=1651s) · 2025-06
  > "Basically my AI timelines are about 5-10X pessimistic w.r.t. what you'll find in your neighborhood SF AI house party or on your twitter timeline" — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1979644538185752935) · 2025-10
  > "I feel like the industry is making too big of a jump and is trying to pretend like this is amazing, and it's not. It's slop." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=2231s) · 2025-10
- Agents will need supervision, and human work moves up to oversight.
  > "these uh models are not infallible as we talked about above so all of this will require supervision" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=11535s) · 2025-02
  > "I think we're going to see something similar in the digital space where we are going to be talking about human to agent ratios" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=11546s) · 2025-02
  > "As LLMs get better, they will do more and more of the legwork autonomously, and a lot more of our work will rise up the abstractions into oversight and understanding." — [x/2026-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479) · 2026-10
- So he ships in steps that are useful early, instead of betting on all or nothing.
  > "How can I set up the product development roadmap so that I'm making revenue along the way? I'm not setting myself up for a zero-one-loss function where it doesn't work until it works." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=6916s) · 2022-10
  > "you can't just like in binary fashion develop it and then ship it" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_g2R2T631x7k_cvpr-20-workshop-on-scalability-in.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g2R2T631x7k&t=1699s) · 2020-06
- Reaching for the full thing too early has failed before.
  > "I feel maybe loosely speaking, people kept trying to get the full thing too early a few times" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=431s) · 2025-10
  > "it was incorrectly sequenced - LLMs had to happen first." — [x/2025-01.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1882544526033924438) · 2025-01

## How it shows up

- He wants a product useful from day one, with the improvement loop built around it, partly to keep the team motivated.
  > "You want to make it useful almost immediately." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=6927s) · 2022-10
  > "you want to set up your data engine, your improvement loops, the telemetry, the evaluation, the harness and everything. And you want to improve the product over time, incrementally." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=6933s) · 2022-10
  > "from the point of view of the team working on it, they need the dopamine along the way." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=6951s) · 2022-10
- In debates about AI he separates people who look at today's level from people who look at the slope.
  > "The majority of the ruff ruff is people who look at the current point and people who look at the current slope." — [x/2026-01.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2008664551445963083) · 2026-01
- He names specific missing pieces instead of waving at "more scale".
  > "the only trick uh the only kind of trick we have Avail to us right now is to make the context Windows longer but I think that that approach by itself will will not will not scale" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=11677s) · 2025-02
- He says plainly where the field has not solved its own basics.
  > "honestly we are getting to The Cutting Edge of where the field is we certainly haven't I would say soled initialization and we haven't soled back propagation" — [youtube/2022-10-09_P6sfmUTpUmc_building-makemore-part-3-activations-gradients.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6sfmUTpUmc&t=6915s) · 2022-10
- He wants calibration because the stakes go beyond tech.
  > "I want this to be properly calibrated, and some of this also has geopolitical ramifications and things like that with some of these questions." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7006s) · 2025-10

## Tensions and evolution

- His own forecasts have been wrong in the pessimistic direction. In 2011 he called 80% on CIFAR-10 hard and later noted it was way off. In 2012 he thought AI was very far away; by 2014 he expected models to beat humans on ImageNet.
  > "My impression from this exercise is that it will be hard to go above 80%" — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2011__04__27__manually-classifying-cifar10.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2011/04/27/manually-classifying-cifar10/) · 2011-04
  > "Obviously this prediction was way off, with state of the art now in 95%" — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2011__04__27__manually-classifying-cifar10.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2011/04/27/manually-classifying-cifar10/) · 2011-04 (2015 update)
  > "In any case, we are very, very far and this depresses me." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2012__10__22__state-of-computer-vision.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2012/10/22/state-of-computer-vision/) · 2012-10
  > "It is clear that humans will soon only be able to outperform state of the art image classification models by use of significant effort, expertise, and time." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2014__09__02__what-i-learned-from-competing-against-a-convnet-on-imagenet.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2014/09/02/what-i-learned-from-competing-against-a-convnet-on-imagenet/) · 2014-09
- Agents that use computers were his own project at early OpenAI, too early then. In the Lex Fridman interview he said it was time to revisit them; in 2025 he still framed the work as a decade.
  > "It just so turns out that this was extremely early, way too early, so early that we shouldn't have been working on that." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=393s) · 2025-10
  > "But now what's happened is it is time to revisit that" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=3049s) · 2022-10
  > "My reaction is we'll be working with these things for a decade. They're going to get better, and it's going to be wonderful." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=95s) · 2025-10
- For coding agents the change came faster than his decade frame suggests. In October 2025 he refused long agent runs; by February 2026 he said agents had worked since December.
  > "I don't want an Agent that goes off for 20 minutes and comes back with 1,000 lines of code." — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1979644538185752935) · 2025-10
  > "coding agents basically didn’t work before December and basically work since" — [x/2026-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2026731645169185220) · 2026-02
