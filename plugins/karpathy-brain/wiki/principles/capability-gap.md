# The capability gap

The gap in shared understanding of what LLMs can do is widening into a funnel, and he names what drives it: problem size, domain, and access.
  > "Dusting off this tweet from April because this gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is *widening*." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "The funnel is driven by a combination of factors:" — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10

## Evidence

- He names three drivers: the size of the problem, the jaggedness of capability across domains, and access.
  > "The impact scales with ambition, problem size, and horizon. A question with a paragraph answer barely stresses the system." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "Capability peaks in domains that are digital, verifiable and economically valuable." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "Access. Free-tier, paid-tier, internal." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- By his napkin math, most people have barely used LLMs, and most of the rest know them as a free chatbot.
  > "Napkin math somewhere around 6B people (~75% of the population) have barely come in contact with LLMs at all." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "This group sees derpy chatbots and treats them a bit like a better Google search, a writing aid, or etc." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- Knowing about it is not the same as seeing it. Many professionals outside math and code know it only second-hand.
  > "so while they understand what is happening intellectually, it is still second-hand and a bit abstract." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "The majority of professionals still see only a modest uplift. And a small sliver of professionals are experiencing the vertigo of the curve going vertical." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- People who use frontier models for math and code see it first-hand: agents finish projects that used to take weeks or months.
  > "Somewhere around 20M people (0.2%) see first-hand that large, complex projects that used to take them weeks/months can now be completed by agents with a prompt." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- Part of the domain gap is where labs put their effort: everyday uses earn less, so they get less attention.
  > "Typical queries around search, writing, advice, etc. are *not* the domain that has made the most noticeable and dramatic strides in capability." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415) · 2026-04
  > "But partly, it's also because these use cases are not sufficiently prioritized by the companies in their hillclimbing because they don't lead to as much $$$ value." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415) · 2026-04
- In February 2025 he told general viewers that the free tier gives a smaller, weaker model, and that thinking models help most on deep, hard problems.
  > "it's not going to be as creative like it's writing might not be as good its knowledge is not going to be as good it's going to probably hallucinate a bit more" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=1148s) · 2025-02
  > "things that are very simple like uh might not actually benefit from this but things that are actually deep and hard might benefit a lot" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=1501s) · 2025-02

## How it shows up

- He does not dismiss people who see little use in it.
  > "I have non-tech friends who (reasonably, imo) say they have not much use for it at all." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- He uses his own coding as an example of how recent the change is.
  > "less than 1 year ago, I was writing code manually by hand, typing memorized computer code commands into a code editor character by character, occasionally pressing Tab to autocomplete a little chunk of code." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
- At the narrow end of the funnel he puts the people with internal access to frontier systems, and says human review and comprehension are starting to fall behind what those systems produce.
  > "And finally we get to the ~5,000 people (~0.00006%) with access to frontier-grade systems internally." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
  > "Meanwhile, human review and comprehension are starting to fall behind." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10

## Tensions and evolution

- April 2026: he described two groups talking past each other. October 2026: he quote-posted the April post, said the gap was widening into a funnel, and split it into four tiers, from people who have barely used LLMs to people inside frontier labs.
  > "Judging by my tl there is a growing gap in understanding of AI capability." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415) · 2026-04
  > "TLDR the people in these two groups are speaking past each other." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415) · 2026-04
  > "and more a sharp funnel." — [x/ingest/2026-10-11_2109361546505966046.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · 2026-10
