# Understanding cannot be outsourced

He hands more and more of the work to AI, but the understanding, the review and the quality bar stay with him.
  > "the LLMs certainly don't excel at understanding. You still are uniquely in charge of that." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1763s) · 2026-04

## Evidence

- The slogan he uses for this is someone else's tweet. In the 2026 Sequoia talk he says so, and he paraphrases it.
  > "there was a tweet that blew my mind recently, and I keep thinking about it like every other day" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1686s) · 2026-04
  > "It was something along the lines of um you can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1689s) · 2026-04
- On X he quote-posted the original tweet (by @yacineMTB) and said he keeps citing it.
  > "This is the the quote I've been citing a lot recently." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2049907410303865030) · 2026-04
- His own reason is that he becomes the bottleneck once agents do the typing.
  > "I feel like I'm becoming a bottleneck of just even knowing what we're trying to build, why is it worth doing, uh how do I direct you know, how do I direct my my agents, and so on." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1706s) · 2026-04
- Models are weak exactly at understanding, so that part stays human.
  > "cuz the LLMs certainly don't excel at understanding. You still are uniquely in charge of that." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1763s) · 2026-04
- Coding is mostly reading and verifying, not writing.
  > "They think that coding is about writing the code (1). It's not. It's about staring at the code (2)." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1930305209747812559) · 2025-06
  > "Generation (writing code) and discrimination (reading code) are different capabilities in the brain." — [x/2026-01.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2015883857489522876) · 2026-01
- So AI help should make verification easier, not just produce more code.
  > "Instead, the LLM has to actively work with you to break down problems into little incremental steps, each more easily verifiable." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1930305209747812559) · 2025-06
- You stay responsible for what the tools produce.
  > "work with them as tools and be ultimately respons responsible for the you know product of your work" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=11368s) · 2025-02
  > "you're still responsible for your software just as before, but can you go faster?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=975s) · 2026-04
- He separates vibe coding from agentic engineering by the quality bar.
  > "I would say vibe coding is about raising the floor for everyone in terms of what they can do in software." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=957s) · 2026-04
  > "But then I would say agentic engineering is about preserving the quality bar of what existed before in professional software." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=966s) · 2026-04

## How it shows up

- He wants to learn while he works with AI, not receive finished code.
  > "I want to learn along the way and become better as a programmer, not just get served mountains of code that I'm told works." — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1979644538185752935) · 2025-10
- He no longer remembers API details, but keeps the fundamentals.
  > "still have to have an understanding of what this stuff is doing and some of the fundamentals" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1298s) · 2026-04
- He hesitates to recommend AI coding to people who cannot read the result.
  > "I'm Amil with the code and I always read it um but I think I would be hesitant to potentially recommend the use of these tools uh if people aren't able to like read it and verify it" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3982s) · 2025-02
- He keeps taste and design for himself.
  > "you're in charge of the taste, the engineering, the design uh, and that it makes sense and that you're asking for the right things" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1311s) · 2026-04
- In nanochat, contributors must flag LLM-written code they don't fully understand.
  > "When submitting a PR, please declare any parts that had substantial LLM contribution and that you have not written or that you do not fully understand." — [github/nanochat/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat/blob/92d63d4e8bb4df75c3b71618f31ddde2378b2bcd/README.md) · 2026-09
- He builds LLM wikis to turn reading into understanding.
  > "I I think that ultimately these are tools to enhance understanding in a certain way." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1749s) · 2026-04

## Tensions and evolution

- In April 2025 he shipped MenuGen without knowing how it works, and said in the same post that this is fine only for unimportant things.
  > "As such, I did not write any code directly; 100% of the code was written by Cursor+Claude and I basically don't really know how MenuGen works in the conventional sense that I am used to." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/) · 2025-04
  > "vibe coding full web apps today is kind of messy and not a good idea for anything of actual importance" — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/vibe-coding-menugen/) · 2025-04
- From late 2025 he treats code itself as disposable, which sits next to his demand to understand it.
  > "Code is ephemeral now and libraries are over, ask your LLM to change it in whatever way you like." — [github/reader3/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/reader3/blob/64960f99d8d3c2eaaec56c6765b4c1aeae14c80b/README.md) · 2025-11
- By March 2026 he stopped fighting agents over code quality.
  > "I'm not very happy with the code quality and I think agents bloat abstractions, have poor code aesthetics, are very prone to copy pasting code blocks and it's a mess, but at this point I stopped fighting it too hard and just moved on." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2035173492447224237) · 2026-03
- In 2026 he describes trusting agent output more, while still disliking what he sees when he looks.
  > "And then I can't remember the last time I corrected it. And then I was I just uh you know, trusted the system more and more." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=91s) · 2026-04
  > "when you actually look at the code, sometimes I get a little bit of a heart attack because it's not like super amazing code necessarily all the time" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1359s) · 2026-04
