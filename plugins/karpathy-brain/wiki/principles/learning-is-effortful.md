# Learning is effortful

Real learning takes effort, like going to the gym; content that feels like learning is often entertainment.
  > "Learning is not supposed to be fun. It doesn't have to be actively not fun either, but the primary feeling should be that of effort." — [x/2024-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1756380066580455557) · 2024-02

## Evidence

- He wants learning to feel like sweat, and says the middle ground fails.
  > "You want the mental equivalent of sweating." — [x/2024-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1756380066580455557) · 2024-02
  > "Attempts to seek the stuff in between actually clamp to zero." — [x/2024-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1756380066580455557) · 2024-02
- He decided that reading a book is entertainment, and that textbooks teach more.
  > "It took me a while to really admit to myself that just reading a book is not learning but entertainment." — [x/2018-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1018544870112165888) · 2018-07
  > "Discovering (paradoxically late in life) that I get more out of textbooks than books" — [x/2018-08.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1031266180240093184) · 2018-08
- He expects school to work like the gym: effortful, chosen, and rewarding.
  > "Education will play out in the same way. You'll go to school like you go to the gym." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7770s) · 2025-10
  > "it's effort but it's also kind of fun and you also have a payoff of like you feel about yourself in various ways right and I think education is basically equivalent to that" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hM_h0UA7upI_no-priors-ep-80-with-andrej.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hM_h0UA7upI&t=2453s) · 2024-09
- Most people don't learn because it is hard and they bounce off the material.
  > "Right now, not that many people learn because learning is hard. You bounce from material." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7776s) · 2025-10
- Knowledge comes from working the material, not from taking it in.
  > "The book is a set of prompts for me to do synthetic data generation, or for you to get to a book club and talk about it with your friends. It's by manipulating that information that you actually gain that knowledge." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=3085s) · 2025-10
  > "practice problems of course we know are critical for learning because what are they getting you to do they're getting you to practice uh to practice yourself and discover ways of solving these problems yourself" — [youtube/2025-10-09_7xTGNNLPyMI_deep-dive-into-llms-like-chatgpt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xTGNNLPyMI&t=8010s) · 2025-02
- He predicts before reading and restates what he learns in his own words.
  > "Ideally never absorb information without predicting it first. Then you can update both 1) your knowledge but also 2) your generative model." — [x/2017-09.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/904807765931024384) · 2017-09
  > "teach/summarize everything you learn in your own words" — [x/2020-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1325154823856033793) · 2020-11
- Expertise is a matter of hours put in, measured only against your past self.
  > "You literally have to put in 10,000 hours of work. It doesn't even matter as much where you put it, you'll iterate and you'll improve and you'll waste some time." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_cdiD-9MMpb0_andrej-karpathy-tesla-ai-self-driving.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdiD-9MMpb0&t=9412s) · 2022-10
  > "only compare yourself to younger you, never to others" — [x/2020-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1325154823856033793) · 2020-11

## How it shows up

- He tells people to skip short content and go for long material.
  > "Consider the opportunity cost of snacking and seek the meal - the textbooks, docs, papers, manuals, longform." — [x/2024-02.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1756380066580455557) · 2024-02
- His lecture videos are meant to be worked through, not watched.
  > "I recommend you work through the exercise yourself but work with it in tandem and whenever you are stuck unpause the video and see me give away the answer. This video is not super intended to be simply watched." — [github/nn-zero-to-hero/README.md](https://github.com/karpathy/nn-zero-to-hero/blob/73c3fcc741f0ec104ca850b1fb0df90e7e8d4cde/README.md) · 2024-08
- He re-explains things to himself as a way of learning them.
  > "I love to re-explain things and people should be doing that more as well. That forces you to manipulate the knowledge and make sure that you know what you're talking about when you're explaining it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8728s) · 2025-10
- He spent more on teaching than his PhD could justify, because the field needed people who could learn it.
  > "I put much more effort into it than is rationally advisable for a PhD student who should be doing research, but I felt that the field was held back if people couldn’t efficiently learn about the topic and enter." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2016__09__07__phd.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/) · 2016-09
- He worries that easy AI output stops the long, effortful work that produces great culture.
  > "It’s a quick release valve that stops deeper work." — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1987926732255719862) · 2025-11
  > "the Tolkien legendarium is imo a concrete example of a height of culture." — [x/2025-08.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1956765908078387382) · 2025-08
- He frames education as how people keep up with AI.
  > "My personal big fear is that a lot of this stuff happens on the side of humanity, and that humanity gets disempowered by it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7062s) · 2025-10
  > "It is imperative that humanity not fall while AI ascends." — [x/2025-08.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1952076108565991588) · 2025-08

## Tensions and evolution

- In 2024 he said learning should not primarily feel fun; in 2025 he said that with the right material it feels good, and that after AGI people will learn for fun.
  > "When you do it properly, learning feels good. It's a technical problem to get there." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=8116s) · 2025-10
  > "I often say that pre-AGI education is useful. Post-AGI education is fun." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7729s) · 2025-10
- By 2025 he read hard material together with an LLM, which lowers the friction he once called necessary.
  > "I feel like llms make a lot of reading very dramatically more accessible than it used to be before because you're not just right away confused" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3489s) · 2025-02
  > "I encourage you to experiment with it and uh don't read books alone" — [youtube/2025-10-09_EWvNQjAaOHw_how-i-use-llms.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWvNQjAaOHw&t=3535s) · 2025-02
