# Andrej Karpathy: how he thinks

He is an AI researcher and teacher. He ran AI at Tesla, founded the education company Eureka Labs, and in 2026 joined Anthropic.
  > "Excited to join Tesla as the Director of AI!" — [x/2017-06.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/877330494555176962) · 2017-06
  > "Excited to share that I am starting an AI+Education company called Eureka Labs." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1813263734707790301) · 2024-07
  > "Personal update: I've joined Anthropic." — [x/2026-05.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2056753169888334312) · 2026-05

## The ideas that explain most of it

1. **Build it to understand it.** He rebuilds things from scratch.
  > "when you build something from scratch, you're forced to come to terms with what you don't understand and you don't know that you don't understand it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=1795s) · 2025-10
2. **The core is tiny.** Strip a system down to the page that holds the real idea.
  > "This file is the complete algorithm. Everything else is just efficiency." — [github/_gists/8627fe009c40f57531cb18360106ce95/microgpt.py](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/8627fe009c40f57531cb18360106ce95) · 2026-02
3. **Neural nets fail silently.** So he trains with a slow, paranoid recipe.
  > "Most of the time it will train but silently work a bit worse." — [blog/karpathy.github.io/2019__04__25__recipe.md](https://karpathy.github.io/2019/04/25/recipe/) · 2019-04
4. **Simplicity is a constraint.** Complexity has to earn its place.
  > "All else being equal, simpler is better. A small improvement that adds ugly complexity is not worth it." — [github/autoresearch/program.md](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch/blob/228791fb499afffb54b46200aca536f79142f117/program.md) · 2026-03
5. **Software 2.0 and 3.0.** Data is the program, and LLMs are a new computer.
  > "LLMs are a new kind of computer, and you program them *in English*." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1935518272667217925) · 2025-06
6. **LLMs are ghosts, not animals.** They need their own mental model.
  > "Stated plainly, today's frontier LLM research is not about building animals. It is about summoning ghosts." — [blog/karpathy.bearblog.dev/animals-vs-ghosts.md](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/animals-vs-ghosts/) · 2025-10
7. **Demos are not products.** Reliability comes one nine at a time.
  > "it's a march of nines. Every single nine is a constant amount of work." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=6359s) · 2025-10
8. **Teaching is building ramps.** He builds live, from an empty file, one step at a time.
  > "In my mind, education is the very difficult technical process of building ramps to knowledge." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=7401s) · 2025-10

## How he changes his mind

He updates in public, with numbers. In October 2025 autocomplete was his sweet spot; by December agents wrote most of his code.
  > "For now, autocomplete is my sweet spot." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_lXUZvyajciY_andrej-karpathy-we-re-summoning-ghosts.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lXUZvyajciY&t=2253s) · 2025-10
  > "I rapidly went from about 80% manual+autocomplete coding and 20% agents in November to 80% agent coding and 20% edits+touchups in December." — [x/2026-01.md](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2015883857489522876) · 2026-01

The understanding stays with him, however much work moves to agents.
  > "I do still think that ultimately something has to direct the thinking and the processing, and so on. And um that's still kind of fundamentally constrained somehow by understanding." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_96jN2OCOfLs_andrej-karpathy-from-vibe-coding-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=96jN2OCOfLs&t=1715s) · 2026-04
