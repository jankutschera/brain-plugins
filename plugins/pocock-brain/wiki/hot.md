# How Matt Pocock thinks

Former voice coach turned TypeScript educator (Total TypeScript), now teaching AI-assisted coding (AI Hero, the mattpocock/skills repo).
  > "My name's Matt, I'm a teacher, and I suppose now I teach AI." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_-QFHIoCo-Ko_full-walkthrough-workflow-for-ai-coding.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QFHIoCo-Ko&t=28s) · AI Engineer

## The ideas that explain most of it

- **Fundamentals matter more with AI.** Old books (Pragmatic Programmer, Ousterhout, DDD, Brooks) are his playbook for agents.
  > "Software engineering fundamentals matter more than ever." — [github/skills/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- **Writing code is cheap; reading and maintaining it is not.** Agents accelerate entropy, so bad code costs more than ever.
  > "Writing code got cheap. Reading it did not." — [blog/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md) · 2026-10
- **Humans own the strategy.** Agents do tactical work; decisions, taste and interfaces stay human.
  > "DO NOT DELEGATE YOUR THINKING" — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1977810532154650870) · 2025-10
- **Align before code.** He has the agent grill him until they share a design concept; specs are disposable by-products.
  > "The most common failure mode in software development is misalignment." — [github/skills/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- **The codebase is the agent's environment.** Deep modules, shared vocabulary and a good harness beat prompt tweaks and model-chasing.
  > "If your code sucks, your agent will too." — [x/2026-08.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2089336734530109837) · 2026-08
- **Context is a scarce budget.** Stay in the "smart zone": one task per session, clear rather than compact, delete most instructions.
  > "It's still 100K of smart, and 900K of dumb." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2034572011175907474) · 2026-03
- **Checks over trust.** Feedback loops, TDD, hooks and deterministic loops beat prose rules; LLM output is unverified until shown.
  > "A check can fail. A sentence in a Markdown file cannot." — [blog/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md) · 2026-10
- **Teacher, not pundit.** He teaches what works now, gives material away and avoids predictions and hype.
  > "I am a teacher. I am not a pundit." — [youtube/2026-05-13_lNOQaakmyDU_anthropic-s-dedicated-monthly-credit-is.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lNOQaakmyDU&t=578s) · 2026-05

## How he changes his mind

He holds definite opinions and reverses them in public, marking the change (plan mode, grilling cadence, types vs interfaces).
  > "I wrote this in January 2026, and my thinking has changed. I no longer use plan mode." — [blog/plan-mode-introduction.md](https://www.aihero.dev/plan-mode-introduction) · 2026-01
