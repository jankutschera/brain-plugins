# Content strategy

Short, free problem/solution pieces win attention ("sugar"); deep courses earn money ("vegetables"); volume and fast feedback beat polish, and questions from the audience are the topic source.
  > "developers love sugar, but they will pay for vegetables." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=632s) · tldraw

## Evidence

- Frame everything as a problem and a solution.
  > "Frame EVERYTHING as a problem and a solution. Videos, courses, articles." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1934939832058523663) · 2025-06
  > "every little video that I do is a problem and a solution." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=703s) · tldraw
- Aim for the moment the viewer's brain explodes.
  > "you are trying to create a moment where their brain explodes." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=550s) · tldraw
- It is a quantity game of small bets.
  > "So it is a it's a quantity game. It's absolutely a quantity game." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1923s) · tldraw
  > "Content is data. The more you put out, the more feedback you get." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1934942357255836078) · 2025-06
- Short beats long on reach.
  > "I know that what works is 2-minute videos." — [youtube/2022-12-20_l2iVQkY6mXQ_i-quit-vercel-to-make-content.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2iVQkY6mXQ&t=247s) · 2022-12
- Help people where they are, and respect their time.
  > "if you're trying to pull people uh off the platform where they are they will not follow you." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=364s) · tldraw
  > "Optimize for skimmability. Use chapters on YT. Summarize articles at the top. Get to the point fast." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1934940308611223616) · 2025-06

## How it shows up

1. Go deep on one thing you know well to find widely useful nuggets, and specialise.
  > "I suggest choosing something that you know really well, getting absolutely deep into it until you find the nuggets of information that are widely useful." — [youtube/2022-12-20_l2iVQkY6mXQ_i-quit-vercel-to-make-content.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l2iVQkY6mXQ&t=281s) · 2022-12
  > "Because you've got to specialize. Um, people need to know you for something, right?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=975s) · tldraw
2. Batch: record many short tips in one sitting and queue them.
  > "I just sat down and recorded 18 two-minute videos on a Sunday and I just like uh, queued them up and I went from 4,000 to 10,000 followers on Twitter." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_8eoiDcVGo80_get-100k-followers-by-teaching-typescript.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eoiDcVGo80&t=555s) · This Dot Media
3. Keep a library of proven topics and recycle winners.
  > "This topic library took a long time to build up. Build yours, then compose these components into content." — [x/2025-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1934941306985255369) · 2025-06
  > "every nine months or so I recycle those." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1072s) · tldraw
4. Threads: a bold promise, small steps, a share ask, then "did I succeed?".
  > "If you don't know generics, I promise you'll understand them by the end of this thread." — [x/2023-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1625838626742435842) · 2023-02
  > "Also - did I succeed? If you didn't understand generics before, does it click for you now?" — [x/2023-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1625848399739355137) · 2023-02
5. Crowdsource questions, names and critique; treat questions as an endless content source.
  > "It's an endlessly renewable resource of new content and cool prompts. What should I do with it?" — [x/2023-01.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1618539859504959493) · 2023-01
  > "Posting for feedback. Which parts are confusing? What questions does it raise?" — [x/2026-07.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2079879414297330146) · 2026-07
6. Show your face, not a logo.
  > "always use your face. People would trust a face a lot more than they would trust a business." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1513s) · tldraw
- He judges by signal, "does number go up", then makes deliberate business choices.
  > "just seeing -does number go up." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=638s) · Sanity
  > "they have to be business decisions in the end 'cause it's my time I'm risking." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=737s) · Sanity

## Tensions and evolution

- He is open that some of it is calculated: he picked TypeScript over XState as the horse to ride.
  > "I noticed that TypeScript was kicking off and I could have gone, \"Nah, I'm gonna choose XState instead.\" So there is an element of calculation there." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=677s) · Sanity
- For AI content he admits he does not know what works.
  > "I have no idea what works. Some stuff works, some stuff doesn't." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1270s) · tldraw
- His growth came without a plan, by pestering a bigger name to retweet.
  > "That that sounds like I had a plan. I didn't have a plan, but that's just how it works." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1991s) · tldraw
