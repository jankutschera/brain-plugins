# Running the business

He gives core material away to build trust and audience, sells products (courses, cohorts, team licences) through a partner platform, launches with bundled "ship days", and optimises for leverage and family time rather than hours.
  > "I like the idea of just being someone who has, okay, these are the products you can buy from me. This is how you can support me." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1348s) · The Pragmatic Engineer

## Evidence

- His model targets education budgets.
  > "if you want to spend some of your education budget on me, that's basically my model." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1363s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- Free content was the plan when he went full-time.
  > "I'm going to double down on free content." — [x/2022-11.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1592467703255924738) · 2022-11
- Paid material is aimed at productivity at work, and later at teams.
  > "The paid version of Total TypeScript is going to be pretty focused on getting you more productive at work." — [x/2022-10.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1586032117620051968) · 2022-10
  > "But with Essentials (the book) and Pro Essentials (the course), I want Total TypeScript to be the default choice for teams." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1810229570949247182) · 2024-07
- His goal has always been leverage: work less, not more.
  > "my goal in hustling, I suppose, was never to work more, it's always to work less." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nFA2oO0IPQs_matt-pocock-how-to-maximize-success.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nFA2oO0IPQs&t=4672s) · Tejas Kumar
  > "I try never to work on weekends basically. I I'm extremely radical about this." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1128s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- He is open about the numbers, up and down.
  > "made $3.5 million right over the course of like two and a bit years." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=110s) · tldraw
  > "So in terms of like my revenue from total time script that's gone down obviously" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1502s) · The Pragmatic Engineer

## How it shows up

1. Hedge first: he kept a part-time Vercel job while testing the course.
  > "I went into it going, \"Okay, I need a stable 9 to5 for 3 days a week while I test this other thing out.\"" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=963s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
2. Partner on the platform side (Joel Hooks, badass.dev) and "chuck it over the wall".
  > "you can just produce the thing and chuck it over the wall and give feedback, work on something else." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=343s) · Sanity
3. Launch with a bundled ship day and goodwill to past buyers.
  > "It's the biggest ship day of my life. Here's everything I'm shipping:" — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1810229563454108158) · 2024-07
  > "Existing TT customers will get Pro Essentials for free." — [x/2024-07.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1810229574975815737) · 2024-07
4. Price with purchasing-power parity and remove friction for expensing.
  > "~$200 (+ PPP). Shipping this month" — [x/2026-08.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2084614149321998532) · 2026-08
  > "Folks who are wanting to expense my course, but not sure how to convince your boss I have a boss letter you can copy/paste:" — [x/2026-08.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2090762514703290775) · 2026-08
5. For AI, run two-week cohorts on top of free open-source skills.
  > "I run courses in two-week cohorts these days because AI moves so quickly." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_rlM_fAKxB3Q_senior-dev-this-grill-me-prompt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rlM_fAKxB3Q&t=47s) · Latent Space
  > "Over 4.2 million downloads and 135,000 GitHub stars later" — [blog/skills__skills-changelog-v1-announcement.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills/skills-changelog-v1-announcement) · 2026-06
- Live workshops sell answers and judgement, not information.
  > "You're not paying for the information." — [blog/events__turn-ai-agents-into-autonomous-software-engineers-with-ralph.md](https://www.aihero.dev/events/turn-ai-agents-into-autonomous-software-engineers-with-ralph) · 2026-02

  > "started to be clear to me that was the wrong bet to make." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1551s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
