# Build your own tools

When a tool he wants does not exist, or annoys him, he builds it, open-sources it and runs it on his own work, and he now treats agent-built personal software as an edge.
  > "It feels crazy to me that there's no TypeScript-native, local-first tool for testing LLM-powered apps." — [blog/evalite-an-early-preview.md](https://www.aihero.dev/evalite-an-early-preview) · 2024-12

## Evidence

- So he built Evalite.
  > "So, I'm building one." — [blog/evalite-an-early-preview.md](https://www.aihero.dev/evalite-an-early-preview) · 2024-12
- "TypeScript annoys me sometimes" became ts-reset.
  > "TypeScript annoys me sometimes. So, I decided to do something about it" — [x/2023-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1627686847030743043) · 2023-02
- He built Sandcastle because alternatives sold a service.
  > "all the tools that I found were trying to sell me some third party service. So, I realized I needed to build something here." — [youtube/2026-04-30_E5-QK3CDVQM_i-open-sourced-my-own-afk.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E5-QK3CDVQM&t=75s) · 2026-04
- He bets on personal software.
  > "Personal software is INSANE in the age of AI" — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1990416710201930104) · 2025-11
  > "the era of personal software is upon us, right?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_d_BuVJgadxs_agent-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_BuVJgadxs&t=2996s) · Unhandled Exception Podcast
- He wants control and ease at once.
  > "I wanted to have control over all of it, but I wanted it to be as easy as possible." — [youtube/2026-08-05_gaDdrDdczO4_new-skills-v1-2-brings-wait.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gaDdrDdczO4&t=477s) · 2026-08

## How it shows up

- His course video manager is his "one-stop shop".
  > "It really is my one-stop shop for everything I need to do my job and it's amazing." — [youtube/2026-03-18_hX7yG1KVYhI_building-a-real-feature-with-claude.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hX7yG1KVYhI&t=104s) · 2026-03
- He wrote all his recent software 100% AI-contributed, on his own projects.
  > "I have been writing all the software that I work on completely 100% AI-contributed for a few months now." — [blog/ways-ai-coding-has-rewired-my-brain.md](https://www.aihero.dev/ways-ai-coding-has-rewired-my-brain) · 2026-03
- He builds small CLIs and skills for repeated chores.
  > "I built a small CLI to ping me on WhatsApp when Ralph finishes." — [blog/tips-for-ai-coding-with-ralph-wiggum.md](https://www.aihero.dev/tips-for-ai-coding-with-ralph-wiggum) · 2026-01
  > "Getting sick of setting up third-party services So I built a skill for it" — [x/2026-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2072042214188847178) · 2026-06
- He runs a Karpathy-style LLM wiki fed by dictated braindumps and an ingest bot.
  > "1. Give each 'deliverable' a Karpathy-style LLM-wiki" — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2097638166232457451) · 2026-09
  > "I have an ingest bot that runs every 3 hours that takes all of that stuff and pulls that into a a wiki." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_d_BuVJgadxs_agent-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_BuVJgadxs&t=3293s) · Unhandled Exception Podcast
- He moved off local dev to an always-on remote box.
  > "the only thing I do locally now is debugging issues with the remote bot." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=4262s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- His first code was tools for his singing students.
  > "I learned to program so I could make interactive exercises for my voice/singing students." — [x/2024-03.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1765036933896356166) · 2024-03
- Across eras he likes generating everything from one source of truth.
  > "**Don't write ten files, write one.** Define your state and actions in a JSON file and watch as your code writes itself." — [github/boilersuit/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/boilersuit/blob/HEAD/README.md)
