# Content production

He produces by improvising and dictating rather than scripting, cuts review cycles, films first and adapts the transcript into articles, and runs the whole pipeline on tools he built and a fixed "Work Clean" routine.
  > "what it really helped me with is just my ability to improvise" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=1792s) · Sanity

## Evidence

- No autocue; he just talks.
  > "I don't have an autocue or anything. I just -talk." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=1810s) · Sanity
- Improvisation is the learnable part of his voice training.
  > "the improvisation thing is really the key thing that I learned. And that's available to anyone, really." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=1838s) · Sanity
- Time spent on a piece is risk, so he cut review cycles early.
  > "the longer you spend on a piece of content, the more risk is inherent in the content, you know?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=509s) · Sanity
  > "Get better at producing content, produce more content, was actually to cut review cycles." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=421s) · Sanity
- He records most articles by dictation.
  > "I record most of my articles via dictation, and this prompt helps me clean up mistakes in the transcription." — [blog/using-mcp-prompts.md](https://www.aihero.dev/using-mcp-prompts) · 2025-03
- He hates waste in his process.
  > "I really hate waste of any kind when I'm working on this stuff" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=181s) · Sanity

## How it shows up

1. Package before producing: a pitch (title, thumbnail, hook) or it is not real.
  > "Lightweight and meant to be killed cheaply — if you can't package a Pitch, you don't have one." — [github/mise-en-place/CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/mise-en-place/blob/HEAD/CONTEXT.md)
2. Plan a course video as beats, then clip mockups played as an animatic, then the script.
  > "It is the first step of the authoring flow: Learning Goals → Lessons, Videos and Beats → Script → recording → article." — [github/course-video-manager/CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/course-video-manager/blob/HEAD/CONTEXT.md)
  > "Opus 5.5 has been building animatics for me - draft versions of videos I'm about to film." — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2105212971307667862) · 2026-09
3. Record with a bad-take button; a pipeline cues up the edit.
  > "I literally have a pipeline where I just press record. It then cues up an edit for me." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=142s) · tldraw
  > "**Bad Take Detection** - Identifies and excludes marked bad takes (triggered by phrases like "that was bad")" — [github/total-typescript-monorepo/docs/video-workflow.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/total-typescript-monorepo/blob/HEAD/docs/video-workflow.md)
4. Film first; articles and newsletters are adapted from the transcript.
  > "Always the first production step — Articles and Newsletters are adapted from the transcript afterwards." — [github/mise-en-place/CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/mise-en-place/blob/HEAD/CONTEXT.md)
5. Run the day in a fixed order: prep before ship.
  > "The morning ritual. **Work Clean**: Prep before Ship, every day, in a fixed order." — [github/mise-en-place/prep-plan.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/mise-en-place/blob/HEAD/prep-plan.md)
6. Read the numbers once a day, time-boxed, and name compulsive checking as a failure mode.
  > "A bounded daily Prep Task: read yesterday's numbers across Channels to maintain a felt sense of what's resonating." — [github/mise-en-place/CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/mise-en-place/blob/HEAD/CONTEXT.md)
  > "Compulsively checking the engagement signal on a freshly-shipped Deliverable in the early window (~first 30 min), before the signal has settled." — [github/mise-en-place/CONTEXT.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/mise-en-place/blob/HEAD/CONTEXT.md)
- He tracks every social post in a personal CMS.
  > "I author every social media post in here, mark when it was posted and if it was a hit." — [x/2024-10.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1846532392367845443) · 2024-10

## Tensions and evolution

- "Never script" in 2022 became dictated scripts cleaned up by AI in 2025.
  > "As soon as folks know you're reading from a script, a part of their brain turns off." — [x/2022-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1565404798249230337) · 2022-09
  > "I've moved to writing scripts via transcription. I basically improvise to camera (not capturing video) as if I'm doing my old way." — [x/2025-01.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1876007690465673679) · 2025-01
- He polishes prose by hand in places: every em-dash in the skills repo is rewritten.
  > "Every em-dash in the repo is hand-rewritten." — [blog/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-changelog-v13-implement-spec-pr-retro-and-glossary-md) · 2026-10
