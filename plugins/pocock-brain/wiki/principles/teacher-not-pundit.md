# Teacher, not pundit

He is a teacher first: he teaches what works now, refuses to predict the future, avoids hype, gives core material away, and treats his audience's trust as the product.
  > "I am a teacher. I am not a pundit." — [youtube/2026-05-13_lNOQaakmyDU_anthropic-s-dedicated-monthly-credit-is.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lNOQaakmyDU&t=578s) · 2026-05

## Evidence

- Teaching predates his dev career and frames how he describes himself.
  > "I have been a teacher for a lot longer than I have been a Dev." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_xk_PbxR7G8A_advanced-typescript-let-s-learn-generics.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xk_PbxR7G8A&t=64s) · CodeTV
  > "My name's Matt, I'm a teacher, and I suppose now I teach AI." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_-QFHIoCo-Ko_full-walkthrough-workflow-for-ai-coding.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QFHIoCo-Ko&t=28s) · AI Engineer
- He refused punditry when entering AI education.
  > "I don't want to be a pundit. I don't want to make hot takes. I don't know the future. I just want to teach you useful skills to get you paid." — [x/2024-12.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1866798098204316106) · 2024-12
  > "I am in the teaching game I'm not in the punditry game in the prediction game and that's where I want to stay because I think predicting the future is a fool's game" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_DCfn1UlSZzs_ai-in-practice-from-cloud-privacy.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DCfn1UlSZzs&t=149s) · Callstack
- He teaches what has always worked, betting it keeps working.
  > "if I do stuff that's always worked, then it will probably continue to work in the future." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=1887s) · David Ondrej
- He calls himself allergic to hype.
  > "I'm someone who's kind of allergic to Hype really" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_5ROCO6Bbdv0_typescript-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ROCO6Bbdv0&t=4899s) · Top End Devs
- Trust obliges accuracy.
  > "at this point they trust what I'm saying to them. So, I need to make sure that what I'm saying is absolutely accurate" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_kJD_1tb6-NE_imposter-syndrome-is-lying-to-you.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJD_1tb6-NE&t=1803s) · Visual Studio Code
- He calls teaching the most rewarding thing he has tried.
  > "Of all the things I've tried to get good at, teaching has been the most rewarding" — [x/2026-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2064417495918727526) · 2026-06

## How it shows up

- He gave away a nine-month book, and free content was the plan from the start.
  > "I wrote a book on TypeScript. It took 9 months of my life. And I gave it away for free." — [x/2025-05.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1922996477254582304) · 2025-05
  > "that's kind of what I'm really passionate about is making the free content available because when I was learning to code that's how I learned" — [youtube/2023-03-07_WuHSnvsO6Mg_total-typescript-tour-and-ama.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WuHSnvsO6Mg&t=309s) · 2023-03
- He wants students to outgrow him.
  > "Don't feel guilty! This is my DREAM end state for my students." — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2099422382368641064) · 2026-09
  > "I have zero incentive for folks to use my actual skills for the rest of their career." — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2105224496864981450) · 2026-09
  > "You won't be dependent on me or anyone else. You'll just know how it works." — [blog/events__turn-ai-agents-into-autonomous-software-engineers-with-ralph.md](https://www.aihero.dev/events/turn-ai-agents-into-autonomous-software-engineers-with-ralph) · 2026-02
- He discloses his incentive.
  > "I sell developer courses right? So I'm sort of, you know, you can take my advice here with a pinch of salt" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=205s) · David Ondrej
- He criticises vendors, including Anthropic, when it hurts learners.
  > "What I care about is that I have to operate as a teacher inside an information vacuum." — [x/2026-04.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2040547504211587293) · 2026-04
- He positions himself as the trustworthy voice in a crowded AI space.
  > "there is a USP there about being the trustworthy voice in a space that's full of" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=834s) · Sanity
- He refuses a heroic origin story and credits luck.
  > "I think I've just been lucky basically of choosing the right thing at the right time." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=5268s) · The Pragmatic Engineer

## Tensions and evolution

- He is frank that content is also a playbook he plays.
  > "it's also a playbook in some sense. I'm also sort of playing the game." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=1094s) · tldraw
- He disliked the algorithmic grind in 2024, then grew fastest on X in 2026.
  > "Social media kind of sucks. I have to lower the quality of what I post to fit the algorithms. I feel stymied." — [x/2024-03.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1769780864844439755) · 2024-03
  > "Gained 1,000 followers every day for 10 days" — [x/2026-01.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2011187491240558869) · 2026-01
- He sometimes wants to stop teaching and go back to engineering.
  > "I would just get back to being an engineer. That's what I love, too." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=5311s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
