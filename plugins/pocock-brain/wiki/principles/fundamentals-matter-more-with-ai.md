# Fundamentals matter more with AI

AI coding does not replace classic software engineering; it raises the stakes on it, and the old books apply harder to agents than they did to humans.
  > "I believe that software fundamentals matter now more than they actually ever have." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_v4F1gFy-hqg_software-fundamentals-matter-more-than-ever.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v4F1gFy-hqg&t=31s) · AI Engineer

## Evidence

- His most-starred repo opens with the thesis.
  > "Software engineering fundamentals matter more than ever." — [github/skills/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- He frames the AI shift as continuity rather than a paradigm shift.
  > "Everyone thinks AI is a paradigm shift. That everything we've learned about building software in the last 20 years is for boomers. I disagree." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2033574421563130048) · 2026-03
- He thinks the best mental models for AI coding are pre-ChatGPT.
  > "I think that the best mental models for building software with AI were figured out 20 years before ChatGPT launched." — [x/2026-03.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2034974175689916571) · 2026-03
- He says the classic principles bind agents harder than they bound humans.
  > "But the principles apply harder to AI than they ever did to humans." — [blog/tracer-bullets.md](https://www.aihero.dev/tracer-bullets) · 2026-01
- He holds that AI coding is not that different from human coding, and that what works for humans transfers.
  > "I really believe that AI coding is not that different from human coding." — [youtube/2026-02-21_yv8VZpov8bk_i-m-using-claude-worktree-for.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yv8VZpov8bk&t=443s) · 2026-02
  > "So, the same techniques that work with humans also, it turns out, work with AI." — [youtube/2026-05-14_6BB6exR8Zd8_i-stopped-using-grill-me-for.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6BB6exR8Zd8&t=801s) · 2026-05
- He sees the industry re-having old conversations with a new participant.
  > "We're just having the same conversations we've had for 20 years. It's just there's this new elephant in the room." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=4975s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- He argues the gap between engineers and vibe coders widens as tools improve.
  > "Fancy tools can't eliminate engineering. In fact, the better the tools get, the wider the gulf gets between vibe coders and real engineers." — [blog/cohorts__ai-coding-for-real-engineers-m0k0w.md](https://www.aihero.dev/cohorts/ai-coding-for-real-engineers-m0k0w) · 2026-06

## How it shows up

- He sends people back to old books instead of new frameworks: The Pragmatic Programmer, Ousterhout, the first chapters of DDD.
  > "I'll go with pragmatic programmer uh philosophy of software design by John Asterau and um I'd say the first like three chapters of DDD" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=5644s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- He mines those books for wording he can paste into prompts.
  > "I've taken tons of stuff that's in this book and just plugged it directly into system prompts and it works great." — [youtube/2026-07-16_t34UuBxB2YQ_my-1-book-recommendation-for-strategic.md](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/t34UuBxB2YQ?t=35s) · 2026-07
- His named techniques are mostly borrowed: deep modules, tracer bullets, ubiquitous language, design concept. Deep modules are his "20-year old solution".
  > "But these problems have a 20-year old solution: deep modules." — [x/2026-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2026960849512870063) · 2026-02
- When asked which fundamentals to learn first, he starts with reading code (reply).
  > "1. Learn to read code 2. Learn to use the terminal 3. Learn how good teams prevent human/agent mistakes (types, tests, linting)" — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2097611379763007870) · 2026-09
- He positions his whole audience around this split: "real engineers", not vibe coders.
  > "This is not for vibe coders. We are people that are serious about AI engineering and serious about building applications that are built to last." — [youtube/2026-03-03_Ah9p7v7nJWg_the-7-phases-of-ai-driven.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ah9p7v7nJWg&t=42s) · 2026-03
- He even built Wayfinder on pre-AI planning practice.
  > "This is based on the fundamentals of planning work that I learned when I was a real developer before AI." — [youtube/2026-07-30_F3lL98Pj90o_wayfinder-nothing-is-too-big-to.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F3lL98Pj90o&t=44s) · 2026-07

## Tensions and evolution

- In 2025 he defended vibe coders; by 2026 his positioning excluded them.
  > "I think vibe coders will be able to fix bugs." — [youtube/2025-05-31_Hw9ytLUln5A_vibe-coders-won-t-be-able.md](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Hw9ytLUln5A?t=21s) · 2025-05
  > "This is not for vibe coders." — [youtube/2026-03-03_Ah9p7v7nJWg_the-7-phases-of-ai-driven.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ah9p7v7nJWg&t=42s) · 2026-03
- He tried the opposite bet, specs-to-code, before settling on fundamentals, and rejected it on results.
  > "as I experimented with it I tried it a lot and I was just getting worse results than if id coded it by hand." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=3128s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- He is candid that the "new" practices are mostly old rules finally enforced.
  > "maybe we're just emphasizing rules that we knew we were supposed to do but maybe we didn't." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=3661s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
