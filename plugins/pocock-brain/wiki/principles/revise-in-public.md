# Revise in public

He holds strong, definite opinions and changes them openly: he marks reversals in old posts, reviews his old videos on camera, and treats his own tools as permanently in progress.
  > "I have very clearly very clear opinions about all this stuff. I don't know if they're good opinions but I have very clear and definite opinions." — [youtube/2026-04-17_K-mA3MZ_EzU_live-watch-me-build-a-brand.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K-mA3MZ_EzU&t=358s) · 2026-04

## Evidence

- He annotates old posts when his view changes.
  > "I wrote this in January 2026, and my thinking has changed. I no longer use plan mode." — [blog/plan-mode-introduction.md](https://www.aihero.dev/plan-mode-introduction) · 2026-01
- He reviews his own old work on camera.
  > "So, I'm going to do something I find deeply uncomfortable, which is look at my old work." — [youtube/2025-04-18_e0AIkYrXAYE_types-vs-interfaces-what-i-got.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e0AIkYrXAYE&t=19s) · 2025-04
- He uses "I was wrong" and "changed my mind" in public posts.
  > "I thought that it would support a limited subset of TS - too limited to be useful. I was wrong." — [x/2022-11.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1593241036267999233) · 2022-11
  > "I've changed my mind on .d.ts files. If you're writing application code, you should NEVER use them." — [x/2024-08.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1820454283457765645) · 2024-08
- X is where he thinks out loud.
  > "I figure it's super beneficial both for me (to figure out what I think) and for you" — [x/2026-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2025568007460729274) · 2026-02
- He is never content with his process.
  > "I'm never very happy when I'm resting on my laurels." — [youtube/2026-05-14_6BB6exR8Zd8_i-stopped-using-grill-me-for.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6BB6exR8Zd8&t=63s) · 2026-05
- He hedged even in 2023.
  > "but I have been proved wrong on that point before" — [youtube/2023-02-01_nwSe95uFN8E_should-you-use-return-types.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nwSe95uFN8E&t=213s) · 2023-02

## How it shows up

- He asks the audience to correct him and treats criticism as input.
  > "What else? Or - what did I get wrong?" — [x/2026-05.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2058457571229327367) · 2026-05
  > "Negative feedback is a gift! Would love to know how to make the skills better. What particularly went wrong?" — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2096979934975565862) · 2026-09
- He ships versioned skills with changelogs and apologises for churn.
  > "Apologies for the churn, but I've been wanting to make this change for a while and now's the time to do it." — [x/2026-07.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2072623114219835733) · 2026-07
- His skill docs record open weaknesses.
  > "This is the sharpest open complaint about wayfinder, and nobody has fixed it yet." — [blog/skills-wayfinder.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-wayfinder) · 2026-07
- He gives a verdict rather than a menu, conditioned on context.
  > "**Verdict**: The more consumers your function has, the more benefit you get from adding a return type." — [github/debates-stream/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/debates-stream/blob/HEAD/README.md)
- He admits when he has no answer.
  > "I don't honestly know what the answer to this yet. I think we just need to be ready to be doing more code review, essentially." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_-QFHIoCo-Ko_full-walkthrough-workflow-for-ai-coding.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QFHIoCo-Ko&t=3602s) · AI Engineer
