# Humans own the strategy

Agents have eaten tactical programming; the human keeps the thinking, the decisions and the long view, and their own skill sets the ceiling on what the agent can do.
  > "Tactical programming is dead Strategic programming has never been more vital" — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2096980473754866081) · 2026-09

## Evidence

- His sharpest early statement on delegation.
  > "DO NOT DELEGATE YOUR THINKING" — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1977810532154650870) · 2025-10
  > "The moment you start delegating your thinking to an LLM, you're screwed." — [blog/personal-software-is-insane-in-the-age-of-ai-u2hx2.md](https://www.aihero.dev/personal-software-is-insane-in-the-age-of-ai-u2hx2) · 2025-11
- He borrows Ousterhout's tactical/strategic split and gives the strategic half to the human.
  > "AI has largely eaten tactical programming in my view and it's up to us to handle the strategic" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1623s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
  > "But they need someone on the level above them who is the strategic programmer." — [youtube/2026-04-29_3MP8D-mdheA_how-to-de-slop-a-codebase.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3MP8D-mdheA&t=578s) · 2026-04
- He thinks agents will not raise strategic problems on their own.
  > "If you tell them to do a task, they will do that task. They aren't RL'd to think outside the box, or propose strategic alternatives." — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2097967205216379355) · 2026-09
- Your own skill caps what the agent can do.
  > "your skills are the ceiling on what AI can do." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=253s) · David Ondrej
  > "If you want to get good at using AI, GET GOOD AT THE THING YOU'RE USING IT FOR" — [x/2026-08.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2083853702696231097) · 2026-08
- He argues the muscle you hand off withers.
  > "the muscle you developed that makes you valuable in society, that you fought so hard to improve, will wither and die." — [x/2025-10.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1977810532154650870) · 2025-10
- His tools are built to empower the user, not the agent.
  > "I think of my skills as empowering the user, not empowering the agent." — [youtube/2026-07-17_32LyZyFQhCQ_framework-hell-tutorial-hell-now-skill.md](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/32LyZyFQhCQ?t=25s) · 2026-07

## How it shows up

- In grilling, facts go to the agent and decisions stay with him.
  > "Decisions are yours, and the agent must wait for them." — [blog/skills-grilling.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-grilling) · 2026-07
- Research is delegated legwork, not outsourced thinking.
  > "Research is legwork you delegate, not thinking you outsource." — [blog/skills-research.md](https://www.aihero.dev/skills-research) · 2026-07
- He keeps product decisions.
  > "I need to be the one making product decisions I know where I'm going" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=2089s) · David Ondrej
- He frames agent work as management: "a lead dev mentality".
  > "Something that I think goes under-emphasized is how much AI coding demands a 'lead dev' mentality." — [x/2026-02.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2026296080602673316) · 2026-02
- He drops the agent when it costs him oversight: course planning went back to pen and paper.
  > "AI seems to be a bad fit (for me) in situations where I need oversight over the entire output at once." — [x/2026-09.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2101303255397494867) · 2026-09

## Tensions and evolution

- He worries this leaves juniors without a path in, and has no settled answer.
  > "But what makes me nervous is that we've pulled down the only bridge that brought juniors into the industry." — [x/2026-05.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2057110441864696164) · 2026-05
  > "how do we train the next level of juniors to think strategically about programming?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_d_BuVJgadxs_agent-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d_BuVJgadxs&t=790s) · Unhandled Exception Podcast
- He is pessimistic about headcount while optimistic about individuals.
  > "it feels to me like just a a nailed on fact that is individual productivity Rises you just need fewer people" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_x18rpm3zGEE_i-quit-voice-coaching-for-typescript.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x18rpm3zGEE&t=2960s) · Backend Banter
  > "I think that there's never been a more empowering time to work on this stuff." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=5424s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
- He says strategic thinking always interested him most, even as a junior, so the shift suits him.
  > "for me, strategic programming has always been the most interesting, the most exciting. That's how I was thinking even when I was a junior." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=86s) · David Ondrej
