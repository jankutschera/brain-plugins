# Learning at the edge

Good teaching pitches each step where the learner is challenged but not intimidated, follows a clear path of prerequisites, and depends on knowing what learners do not know more than on expertise.
  > "teach someone in the area where they are perfectly challenged but not intimidated." — [youtube/2026-06-08_s5T5oQJcJ6U_learn-anything-with-the-teach-skill.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=442s) · 2026-06

## Evidence

- The zone of proximal development is the idea he is "obsessed with".
  > "The zone of proximal development is this key teaching idea which I am obsessed with." — [youtube/2026-06-08_s5T5oQJcJ6U_learn-anything-with-the-teach-skill.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=435s) · 2026-06
- Knowing what learners don't know matters more than expertise.
  > "the useful thing when you're a teacher is not knowing the thing. It's knowing what learners don't know." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=781s) · tldraw
  > "the beginner eyes I would say are much more valuable than the expertise." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=836s) · tldraw
- AI made knowledge cheap; wisdom has not got easier.
  > "knowledge is now very cheap to acquire right very very cheap you can just look it up" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1480s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
  > "wisdom is almost impossible to obtain without actually having done the thing in the exact context where you need to do it." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=1450s) · David Ondrej
- Difficulty hurts when acquiring knowledge and helps when building skills; retention beats fluency.
  > "For acquiring knowledge, difficulty is the enemy. It eats working memory you need for understanding." — [github/skills/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/HEAD/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md)
  > "Fluency can give the user an illusory sense of mastery, but storage strength is the real goal." — [github/skills/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/HEAD/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md)
- Teaching is turning a graph of knowledge into a linear path.
  > "turning that graph into a linear path is how I think of my job, right?" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_4DhcSPkEbwI_ai-skills-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=5139s) · The Pragmatic Engineer
  > "1. Turn the information into a DAG 2. Assign groups to areas of the graph 3. Figure out a linear way through those groups" — [x/2022-11.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1590313564992339969) · 2022-11
- Learning is orientation toward a mission, not information transfer.
  > "I think of teaching and learning as not getting information into your head but orienting you in the world, putting you in a new place in the world." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_nQwJVHCtDDY_matt-pocock-s-agentic-engineering-workflow.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQwJVHCtDDY&t=566s) · David Ondrej

## How it shows up

- His teach skill starts from the learner's why.
  > "I believe that for a teacher to be effective, you need to understand why a student wants to learn the thing." — [youtube/2026-06-08_s5T5oQJcJ6U_learn-anything-with-the-teach-skill.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=167s) · 2026-06
- He hunts for the single example that makes a concept click.
  > "Sometimes, all it takes to teach something is to find the perfect example" — [x/2025-11.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/1994475432763113845) · 2025-11
- He keeps a folder of learner confusions.
  > "I actually have a folder in my, um, sort of repo of like just confusions, of people getting confused by things." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_hQDSr7uwu6E_the-future-of-developer-content-matt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hQDSr7uwu6E&t=1155s) · Sanity
- He credits his lack of a CS background for staying close to learners.
  > "not having the curse of knowledge has benefited me in weird ways too making me closer to my Learners" — [youtube-appearances/unknown_5ROCO6Bbdv0_typescript-with-matt-pocock.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5ROCO6Bbdv0&t=841s) · Top End Devs
- Even in 2022 he modelled docs as a dependency graph so readers meet prerequisites first.
  > "On each page, they referenced pieces of information that the viewer hadn't understood yet." — [github/graph-docs-cli/README.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/graph-docs-cli/blob/HEAD/README.md)

## Tensions and evolution

- He put ten years of teaching into an AI skill, yet does not want learners hooked on the agent.
  > "I poured my 10 years of teaching experience into a skill. It's called /teach, and it can teach you anything." — [x/2026-06.md](https://x.com/mattpocockuk/status/2064023481398824993) · 2026-06
  > "My idea with this teach skill is not to have you hooked onto the agent for learning everything." — [youtube/2026-06-08_s5T5oQJcJ6U_learn-anything-with-the-teach-skill.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=577s) · 2026-06
- He called prompt-based exercises the future of courses, then found AI-experimental formats put people off.
  > "And actually, I think I genuinely think it's the future of courses." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_CLw3Oj13_bA_matt-pocock-good-content-for-developers.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CLw3Oj13_bA&t=194s) · tldraw
  > "the more I lean into the kind of AI experimental stuff, the more it actually turns people off my materials." — [youtube-appearances/unknown_rlM_fAKxB3Q_senior-dev-this-grill-me-prompt.md](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rlM_fAKxB3Q&t=848s) · Latent Space
